Open-source research software · Early stage

Independent checks for computational energy research.

Open Energy Methods develops reproducible open-source tools for assessing specified physical and numerical properties of engineering-model outputs. EnerLint is our first experimental toolkit; it does not replace model validation or safety review.

Scope

What we do, and what we do not

We build tools to make specific computational checks inspectable and repeatable. Good engineering still requires judgment beyond any automated report.

We do

  • +Build transparent, reusable methods for checking stated physical and numerical properties of model outputs.
  • +Publish benchmarks, including deliberately faulty cases and a known case the checks cannot catch.
  • +Report the boundary, assumptions, tolerances and limitations alongside each result.
  • +Develop in the open: accessible source, tests, documentation and issues.

We do not

  • −Replace a solver, modelling framework or physical-property library.
  • −Certify a model, sign off a design, or guarantee correct engineering conclusions.
  • −Claim experimental validation, industrial qualification or safety suitability.
  • −Treat a conservation pass as proof that inputs, assumptions or measurements are correct.

One library, in early alpha

A deliberate starting point: thermal conservation and storage accounting with precisely declared scope, not a broad catalog of unimplemented software.

EnerLint

Experimental alpha
An Open Energy Methods project · v0.1.0a0 · MIT licence

Independent, scope-limited physical consistency checks for exported energy-engineering results—separate from the originating solver's convergence flag.

  • +Mass-flow and first-law energy closure for steady control volumes, with explicit heat and work signs.
  • +Stored-energy accounting for one thermal-storage interval.
  • +Strict JSON and SI input contracts; unknown or missing fields are not silently interpreted as zero.
  • +Machine-readable results, input fingerprint, scoped status and documented limitations.

Evidence first

A result is only as strong as its reference

The categories below describe a research progression, not recognized accreditation levels. EnerLint currently supports scoped exported-data residual checks; it has not achieved empirical validation or engineering certification.

Stage 0Native solver status

Solver success does not, by itself, supply independent evidence.

Stage 1 · TodayExternal residual checks

Defined balances on exported data and declared boundaries.

Stage 2Independent reference

Separately justified analytical or manufactured benchmarks.

Stage 3Cross-tool comparison

Harmonized results from another implementation.

Stage 4Measured validation

Comparison against independent measurements and uncertainty.

Contribute

The most useful contribution right now is scrutiny

The mathematical assumptions, unit conventions, tolerances and hand-built benchmark choices need independent critique. Reproduce a check, challenge an assumption, report a missed defect, or propose a permission-cleared reference case.
